These 15 places are in the delivery tariff but are not on any map.
The shop cannot measure the road to them, draw them on the dispatch
map, or check that their price makes sense against their neighbours.

To fix one: stand there, or ask somebody who lives there, and send
the WhatsApp location pin. Then write the two numbers below, or just
send the pin on.

* Klien Pouderoyen matters most. It is the cheapest west-side price
  in the tariff, so the cost of the bridge is worked out from it, so
  it quietly sets the fare of every trip between two west-side
  places. It is the only West Bank price still resting on a guess.

Seven of the fifteen are on the west side. Four (Dora, Long Creek,
Loo Creek and the rest) are far out on the Soesdyke-Linden road and
can wait.

A landmark helps as much as a pin: "Sisters Village Public Road"
can be found by a map service when "Sisters" alone cannot.
